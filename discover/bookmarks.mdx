Bookmarks let you save an opportunity without adding it to your pipeline. Use them for opportunities you want to keep visible while you decide whether to pursue them.

## How Bookmarks work

* **Save with one click.** Bookmark any opportunity from search results or its detail page.
* **Shared with your team.** Bookmarks appear in a dashboard visible to your whole organization, which avoids two people triaging the same opportunity.
* **Convert when ready.** When you decide to bid, convert the bookmark into a pursuit with one click. The opportunity moves into your [Pursuits pipeline](/pursuits/dashboard) with its documents attached.

## When to bookmark vs. pursue

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Looks interesting, needs triage | Bookmark |
| Waiting on more information (amendment, Q&A responses) | Bookmark, and add a [Monitor](/intelligence/monitors) for updates |
| You've decided to bid, or need to track capture work | Add as a pursuit |

This split keeps pipeline metrics accurate: pursuits contain only opportunities your team is actively working, and bookmarks hold everything else.
