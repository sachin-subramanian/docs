Federal Market Intelligence is Sweetspot's research layer over federal spending data from USASpending, FPDS, and SAM. It covers more than 167 million awards. Use it to find who holds the work today, what they were paid, when it recompetes, and how an agency buys.

## Federal Award AI

Ask spending questions in plain English and get answers with charts, insights, and exportable results:

```
Show me SDVOSB IT contracts over $5M awarded by the VA in the last two years
```

The AI translates your question into a query over the awards dataset and returns results you can drill into and export.

The same questions work in [Ask AI](/ai/ask-ai), if chat is your preferred way to work. Ask there when you want to combine award data with other context, like your Library or an opportunity's documents, in one conversation; use Federal Award AI when you want a dedicated research surface with charts and exports.

## Recipients

Research any contractor or grantee. Search by name, UEI, DUNS, or CAGE code, and filter by socioeconomic certifications (8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, SDVOSB), NAICS, PSC, agency, location, and spending range.

<img src="/images/features/fmi-recipients.png" alt="A recipient profile in Sweetspot with insights and award cash flow" />

Each recipient profile shows total obligations, cash-flow trends, agency mix, set-aside participation, past awards, and parent/subsidiary hierarchies. Export result sets to Excel or CSV.

Use Recipients to research incumbents before a bid, find teaming partners with verified past performance, and track competitors.

## Award Details

Every contract and grant has a detail page: transaction history, obligation timeline, period of performance, and sub-awards. Linked solicitations connect historical awards to live opportunities, which surfaces recompetes before a new solicitation posts.

<img src="/images/features/fmi-award-details.png" alt="An award detail page in Sweetspot with funding progress and key dates" />

## Forecasts

Agency procurement forecasts list planned purchases, often 12 or more months before an RFP posts. Filter by agency, NAICS, and set-aside. Each forecast has a detail page with the timeline, estimated dates, value, and points of contact.

<img src="/images/features/fmi-forecasts.png" alt="A forecast detail page in Sweetspot with timeline, dates, and contacts" />

## Budgets

Search federal budget accounts to see where funding is growing or shrinking before it's obligated. Each account shows the budget lifecycle (requested, enacted, appropriated, obligated, outlaid), how much of it is addressed to vendors, and year-over-year trends.

<img src="/images/features/fmi-budgets.png" alt="Federal budget account search in Sweetspot" />

## Protests

Search GAO bid protest decisions. Each protest shows the protester, agency, solicitation, filing and decision dates, and the outcome (denied, dismissed, or sustained).

<img src="/images/features/fmi-protests.png" alt="GAO bid protest search in Sweetspot" />

## Contract Vehicles

Research GWACs, IDIQs, and Schedules. Each vehicle page shows total obligated, ceiling, and remaining amounts, the full awardee list, task orders, and analytics on obligations, market share, agencies, set-asides, competition, and geography.

<img src="/images/features/fmi-vehicles.png" alt="A contract vehicle page in Sweetspot with awardees and obligation analytics" />

## NAICS

Every NAICS code has its own workspace showing market size, year-over-year momentum, and vendor count, with tabs for top buyers, incumbents, recompetes, open opportunities, and peer codes. The Recompetes tab lists expiring contracts with obligated amounts, remaining ceiling, and days left, so you can find recompete targets in your industry.

<img src="/images/features/fmi-naics.png" alt="A NAICS code workspace in Sweetspot showing expiring contracts" />

## PSC

PSC codes get the same workspace, opening with a market brief: market size over the last 12 months, momentum, incumbent concentration, and how much of the market is set aside for small business, plus obligations by fiscal year and which agencies are driving the change.

<img src="/images/features/fmi-psc.png" alt="A PSC code workspace in Sweetspot with market brief and money flow" />

## Agency dashboards

Every agency has a dashboard with fiscal-year obligations, contract actions, small business set-aside percentage, and grant awards, plus a monthly spending trend, award type mix, top recipients, and NAICS and PSC breakdowns.
