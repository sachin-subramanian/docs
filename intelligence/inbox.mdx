The Inbox collects monitor alerts, incoming GSA eBuy RFQs, and Saved Search matches in one feed. It sits at the top of the sidebar, with an unread indicator when something new arrives.

## Sources

* **Monitors.** Every alert from your [Monitors](/intelligence/monitors): new awards, amendments, agency activity, and web mentions.
* **Saved Searches.** New opportunities matching your [Saved Searches](/discover/saved-searches).
* **GSA eBuy.** If your organization has the eBuy integration enabled, RFQ notifications forwarded to your unique Sweetspot ingest address appear here automatically with their attachments. See [Integrations](/settings/integrations).

## Triage

Tools for working through the feed:

* Filter by monitor, alert type, and status
* Full-text search across alerts
* Star, archive, mark read/unread, and dismiss, individually or in bulk
* A detail view per alert with a lineage timeline showing the history of changes that led to it

From any alert you can jump straight to the underlying opportunity, award, or vendor, ask [Ask AI](/ai/ask-ai) to qualify it, or add it to your [Pursuits](/pursuits/dashboard).

<Tip>
If you keep dismissing the same kind of alert, tighten that monitor's filters instead. See [Monitors](/intelligence/monitors).
</Tip>
