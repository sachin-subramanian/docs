Sweetspot is an AI platform for government contractors. It brings opportunity discovery, federal market intelligence, pipeline management, and proposal writing into one secure workspace at [console.sweetspotgov.com](https://console.sweetspotgov.com).

This guide covers every part of the console, organized the way the product is: **Search**, **Ask AI**, **Pursuits**, **Library**, and your **Inbox**.

## Set up

Start by giving Sweetspot context about your business. Search results, capture briefs, and drafts all use your Library and company profile as input.

## Security

Sweetspot is SOC 2 Type II audited and CMMC 2.0 Level 2 certified, with FedRAMP Moderate Authorization in progress. AI requests are served from U.S. data centers with zero data retention, and your data never trains third-party models. Role-based access control and SSO are available for enterprise organizations.

For additional security information, request access to our trust center at [trust.sweetspot.so](https://trust.sweetspot.so/).

## Getting help

Start with this guide: the answer to most how-do-I questions is on the page covering that feature, and the search bar above works across every page. If you're still stuck, click **Live Support** in the console to chat directly with the Sweetspot team. Questions go straight to real people, and response times are fast.
