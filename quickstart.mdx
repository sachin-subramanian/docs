This walkthrough covers the core Sweetspot workflow: give the AI context about your business, find an opportunity, qualify it, and start pursuing it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your business info in Settings">
    Go to **Settings** and fill in your organization's basics: name, website, description, UEI and CAGE code, and address under **Overview**, and your NAICS codes, PSC codes, and set-asides under **Classifications**. These drive opportunity matching and AI recommendations. See [Settings](/settings/settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your Library">
    Go to **Library → Files** and upload the documents that describe your business: past proposals, capability statements, key personnel resumes, and pricing sheets. Sweetspot indexes them and uses them in search results, capture briefs, and drafts.

    <Tip>
    If your content lives in SharePoint, connect it under **Settings → Integrations** and sync folders directly instead of uploading files one at a time. See [Integrations](/settings/integrations).
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for opportunities">
    Open **Search** and describe what you're looking for in plain language, for example:

    ```
    IT modernization contracts with the Navy over $5M
    ```

    Sweetspot searches federal contracts, federal grants, DIBBS, SBIR/STTRs, and state and local sources. Use filters for structured criteria like NAICS codes, set-asides, or solicitation numbers. See the [Search guide](/discover/search).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Qualify with Ask AI">
    Open any opportunity and click **Ask AI**. Ask about requirements, evaluation criteria, incumbents, or fit:

    ```
    Does my company qualify for this opportunity? What gaps do we have?
    ```

    Answers are cited to the solicitation documents. Generate a capture brief for a structured bid/no-bid recommendation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to your Pursuits">
    To pursue an opportunity, add it as a **Pursuit**. It enters your pipeline, where you can assign owners, track tasks and deadlines, take notes, and move it through stages from pursuit to proposal to won or lost. See [Pursuits](/pursuits/dashboard).

    Not ready to commit? [Bookmark it](/discover/bookmarks) instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automate your discovery">
    Save your best queries as [Saved Searches](/discover/saved-searches) to get alerted when new matches post, and set up [Monitors](/intelligence/monitors) to watch specific agencies, competitors, and opportunities. New activity lands in your [Inbox](/intelligence/inbox).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft the proposal">
    Sweetspot automatically shreds the solicitation into requirements when files are added to a pursuit. Review the compliance matrix under **Requirements**, then generate an outline and first draft in **Proposal Documents**. Export to a branded Word document when you're ready. See the [Proposal Engine guide](/proposals/proposal-engine).
  </Step>
</Steps>
